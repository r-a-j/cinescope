# Media Details & Streaming Availability

Tapping any poster card across CineScope opens a comprehensive detail view offering production metadata, cast and crew filmographies, multi-season episode guides, and real-time streaming availability.

---

## Movie Details View

The movie details screen brings together essential information in a clean, visual layout:

<div align="center">
  <img src="/img/screenshots/04-movie-details.jpg" width="340" alt="Movie Details View in CineScope" />
  <p><sub><b>Figure 1:</b> Detailed movie view displaying backdrop imagery, certification badges, runtime, genres, user vote averages, overview synopsis, and full cast carousel.</sub></p>
</div>

### Information Displayed

- **Visual Header**: High-definition backdrop, official title poster, and original title.
- **Key Metrics**: Release year, content rating/certification (e.g., PG-13, R), total runtime in hours and minutes, and user rating score out of 10.
- **Genre Pills**: Interactive badges representing film genres (e.g., *Action*, *Adventure*, *Science Fiction*).
- **Synopsis & Tagline**: Official studio summary and promotional tagline.
- **Cast & Crew Carousels**: Scrollable portrait cards with actor names and character roles. Tapping any actor navigates to their biographical summary and filmography.

## TV Series Details & JustWatch Streaming Availability

Television series feature dedicated hubs with real-time streaming provider availability powered by JustWatch data.

  <img src="/img/screenshots/06-tv-series-details.jpg" width="340" alt="TV Series Details and Streaming Availability" />

### Streaming Providers (JustWatch Integration)

Never wonder where to watch a movie or series:
- **Subscription Services**: Direct indicators for platforms included in subscriptions (e.g., Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, Max, Hulu).
- **Free Streaming**: Ad-supported free streaming platforms.
- **Rent & Purchase**: Digital storefronts where the title can be rented or bought (Apple TV, Google TV, Amazon Video).
- **Country Localization**: Streaming data adjusts based on regional availability.

## Interactive TV Season & Episode Guide

For television series, CineScope provides an in-depth episode browser:

<div align="center">
  <img src="/img/screenshots/07-tv-episodes-guide.jpg" width="340" alt="TV Episodes Guide in CineScope" />
  <p><sub><b>Figure 3:</b> Interactive episode guide displaying season selection, episode air dates, episode titles, individual community ratings, and episode descriptions.</sub></p>

### Features of the Episode Guide

- **Season Selector**: Easily switch between Season 1, Season 2, or special feature releases.
- **Episode Stills**: High-resolution screenshot still for each individual episode.
- **Episode Metrics**: Episode number, air date, and independent community vote rating.
- **Narrative Synopsis**: Spoiler-safe episode storyline summaries.
- **Director & Guest Star Badges**: Notable guest cast appearances and episode director credits.
