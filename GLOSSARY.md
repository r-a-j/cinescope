# CineScope

CineScope helps people discover films and series and keep track of what they plan to watch or have watched.

## TV Tracking

**Episode progress**:
The episodes a person has individually marked watched or unwatched.

**Series completion**:
A person's declaration that they have watched all currently known regular episodes of a series. New episode information can reopen the series as in progress.

**Special episode**:
An episode categorized separately from regular seasons. It can be tracked individually but does not count toward regular episode progress or series completion.

**Watched**:
A library status indicating that a person considers a movie or series watched. For a series, this is distinct from its episode-by-episode progress.
